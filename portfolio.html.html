about

A builder who's now studying how engineering gets led.

I'm a final-year Computer Engineering student at MKSSS's Cummins College of Engineering, Pune, graduating in 2026. Most of my work sits at the intersection of three things: full-stack development, applied machine learning, and self-taught UI/UX design.

During my internship at TresVista Analytics, I helped build a microservices architecture with a Java backend and React dashboards — real APIs, real deployments, real production constraints. Outside of internships, I've shipped computer-vision safety systems, churn-prediction models, and a Figma-based accessibility case study for a food-delivery app.

I'm now applying to Master of Engineering Management programs, because the pattern across TresVista, BloomCycle, and my own projects kept pointing the same way: I like being close to the build, but I'm just as drawn to the decisions around it — scoping, prioritising, and leading the people making it happen. I'm open to internships in that space alongside fresher SWE/AI-ML roles.

Outside the IDE, I organise events and mentor a DSA series at LOOP, curate speakers for TEDxCCOEW, and co-founded BloomCycle, which won first place in a business idea competition.
